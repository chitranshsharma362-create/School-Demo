OUR STORY · 01

We began with one question:
what should school feel like?

Rivermount was imagined as a place where children are known before they are measured.

2009

A place to belong before a place to achieve.

Rivermount began as a small learning community with a belief that remains unchanged: children learn best when they feel safe enough to be themselves and supported enough to try something difficult.

We have grown, but our philosophy has stayed personal. Every major decision returns to a simple test—does this create a better environment for a young person to grow?

WHAT GUIDES US

Five principles. Quietly practiced every day.

01CuriosityQuestions are not interruptions to learning. They are where it begins.
02KindnessAchievement means more when we know how to bring others with us.
03CourageGrowth asks us to begin before we are completely ready.
04DepthWe value understanding over simply finishing quickly.
05BelongingEvery child should know that their presence matters here.
FROM OUR PRINCIPAL

Confident enough to try. Safe enough to ask. Supported enough to recover when something is difficult.

Priya Sharma
Principal, Rivermount International School
